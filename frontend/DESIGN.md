# Ahead interface

The public pages explain the purpose before opening the workspace. The workspace uses simple questions: which dates, which numbers, and how far ahead. Small information buttons open short explanations without filling the screen with instructions.

Dark neutral surfaces and thin borders keep the charts central. Green marks predictions and primary actions. White shows known numbers. Fonts are local Geist Sans and Geist Mono. Controls collapse on mobile.

The past-data check shows average miss and how often the real answer fell inside the range. Technical sMAPE and MASE scores sit under More details. Keyboard chart inspection, native date controls, range sliders, visible focus states and reduced-motion support remain available.

The prediction stays visible after settings change, with a Needs updating notice. Export is disabled until settings and result match. Data-help links open separately so they do not clear the current workspace.

Public routes are prerendered for readable HTML and page-specific SEO. Local builds remain noindex until a real HTTPS SITE_URL is supplied.
